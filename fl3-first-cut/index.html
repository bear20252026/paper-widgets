Lamborghini Trattori · 1961–1967

LamborghiniFL3

Three cylinders. No radiator.

Before the supercars, Lamborghini built tractors. The 2R of 1961 carried this engine: 2,193 cc of direct-injection diesel and no radiator, cooled by a belt-driven blower and cast fins. About 2,000 were sold by 1967, and tractor money paid for the move into cars in 1963.
