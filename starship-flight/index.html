STARSHIP

Two stages. One vehicle. No throwaway hardware.

Starship is the largest launch system ever flown. A 72 m booster with 33 methane engines pushes a 52 m reusable upper stage through max Q, releases it hot, and then turns around to be caught by the tower it launched from. The whole stack is designed to fly again. This page runs a modelled flight, not a mission.
